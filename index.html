Case studies

Real reports, real clients, real numbers.

Restaurant

Cutting no-shows without losing bookings

Problem

A 40-seat neighborhood restaurant was losing an estimated 15 covers a week to no-shows on weekend reservations, with no way to tell which bookings were at risk.

Approach

Analyzed 14 months of reservation data to identify the booking patterns most associated with no-shows — party size, lead time, and channel — and built a simple risk flag the host stand could use.

Result

Targeted confirmation calls for high-risk bookings only, instead of everyone, cutting staff time spent on confirmations while reducing no-shows.

No-show rate11% → 6%
Recovered covers / month+58
Confirmation calls needed–70%
Gym / studio

Finding the churn window before it happens

Problem

A boutique studio had steady sign-ups but couldn't tell which new members were likely to cancel before their first renewal.

Approach

Built a simple early-warning model from check-in frequency and class-booking behavior in the first three weeks of membership.

Result

Staff now reach out to flagged members in week two with a class recommendation instead of a generic check-in email.

90-day churn–22%

Café

Fixing a menu that looked fine on paper

Problem

A three-location café chain wanted to simplify its menu but didn't know which items were actually driving profit versus just taking up prep time.

Approach

Combined POS sales data with estimated prep time and ingredient cost per item to rank the full menu by margin-per-labor-minute.

Result

Cut 9 low-performing items, reworked two combos around high-margin items, and simplified prep during peak hours.

Menu items removed9
Peak-hour ticket time–19%
Gross margin+4.3 pts
